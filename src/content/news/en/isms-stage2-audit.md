## November 2026 Update: Certification Obtained

In November 2026, Cor.Inc. obtained ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (ISMS) certification. The scope covers "custom software development and operations."

The certification body is SGS Japan (SGS), and the registration number is JP26/00000364. The validity period is from November 7, 2026, to November 7, 2029. It remains valid subject to satisfactory surveillance audits during the validity period.

Below is the announcement published on October 20, 2026, prior to certification registration.

---

## Completion of the Stage 2 Audit

On October 7, 2026, Cor.Inc. completed the Stage 2 audit for ISO/IEC 27001, the international standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS). No non-conformities were identified during the audit.

Registration of the certification is expected around December 2026. Until registration is completed, we will not state that we are "certified," and we will make another announcement once registration is finalized.

## Future Initiatives

We support the creation of AI environments that handle information that is difficult to share with external AIs, such as contracts and customer data. We will continue to strengthen our internal structure to safeguard our clients' information.

Our initiatives regarding information security are summarized in our [Security Policy](/security).
