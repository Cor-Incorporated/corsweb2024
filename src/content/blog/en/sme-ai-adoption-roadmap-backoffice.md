# Why AI Adoption Stalls in SMEs | A Practical Roadmap Starting from General Affairs and Administration

**For AI adoption in SMEs, start with daily document workflows rather than flashy automation**

AI adoption among small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) is not as advanced as news coverage suggests. While adoption rates are rising, many companies face hurdles such as "not knowing what to use it for," "lacking talent," and "concerns about whether it is safe to input our data." That is precisely why the realistic first step is not a large-scale AI system, but rather document workflows in general affairs and administrative departments.

At Cor.Inc., we believe that AI adoption for SMEs is not about "following a trend," but a practical measure against labor shortages and over-reliance on individual tacit knowledge. Even without dedicated specialist departments like those in large enterprises, you can verify its effectiveness by starting with daily tasks such as meeting minutes, contracts, price quotes, and inquiry responses.

> **Objective Data Informing This Article**
> - In a March 2026 survey by SMRJ (Organization for Small & Medium Enterprises and Regional Innovation, JAPAN), the AI adoption rate among SMEs was 20.4%, with 18.6% considering adoption.
> - In the same survey, when looking at the adoption rate by operational area among companies that have already introduced AI, general affairs and administrative departments were the highest at 68.3%.
> - IPA's DX Trends 2025 notes that in Japan, smaller enterprise sizes correlate with lower engagement rates in generative AI, with nearly 80% of companies with 100 or fewer employees falling under the combined categories of "interested, but have no plans" or "no plans for the future."

## Why AI Adoption Stalls Easily in SMEs

The reason is not merely technical difficulty. Rather, bigger reasons are that it remains undecided which workflows to start with, who will take ownership, and which information can safely be fed into AI.

When AI adoption is framed as an "enterprise-wide transformation," it becomes overwhelming. Initially, focus should be narrowed to operations where time savings are readily visible and information handling is easy to organize.

## Why Start with General Affairs and Administrative Departments

General affairs and administrative departments concentrate document workflows that are highly compatible with AI: meeting minutes creation, internal regulations search, contract first-read reviews, answering inquiries, expense and billing-related verifications, and manual maintenance.

While these place a heavy burden on front-line staff, their results are easy to measure. You can assess outcomes such as how many minutes were cut from meeting minutes creation, how many internal inquiries were reduced, and how much faster the initial action on contract reviews became as a result of adopting AI.

## 90-Day Roadmap

- Weeks 1–2: Determine target workflows, permissible input data, and AI tools to use.
- Weeks 3–4: Pilot with one workflow, such as meeting minutes, internal FAQs, or contract first-read reviews.
- Weeks 5–8: Gather usage logs, time savings, and front-line feedback/corrections.
- Weeks 9–12: Differentiate workflows suitable for cloud AI from those where local LLMs or isolated environments should be considered.

Following this order allows you to generate the evidence needed to make decisions on AI adoption without committing to large systems upfront. Cor.Inc. designs realistic adoption steps spanning document workflows in general affairs and administration, internal knowledge, confidential data utilization, and AI training.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q. Which operations should SMEs start with when adopting AI?

It is realistic to begin with document-heavy operations where results are easy to measure, such as creating meeting minutes, internal FAQs, contract first-read reviews, organizing price quotes, and responding to inquiries.

### Q. Is specialized talent required to adopt AI?

It is not strictly required for the initial validation. However, at stages involving confidential information or integration with internal systems, design involving external specialists becomes necessary.

### Q. How should cloud AI and local LLMs be chosen and used?

Use cloud AI for public information and general documents, and consider local LLMs or approved environments for confidential customer data, personal information, internal documents, and the like.
